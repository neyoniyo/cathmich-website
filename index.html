ABOUT CATHMICH

Technology expertise with a practical business focus.

Cathmich Consulting Ltd. is a Calgary-based Canadian technology solutions and consulting company. We work with organizations that need a dependable partner to help navigate technology procurement, data initiatives, cloud solutions and IT delivery.

Our approach starts with the requirement—not the product. We focus on understanding business needs, identifying practical options and supporting solutions that are scalable, fit for purpose and aligned with long-term objectives.

Our experience includes supporting Canadian education and enterprise environments, where reliability, clear communication, responsible delivery and attention to technical requirements matter.
